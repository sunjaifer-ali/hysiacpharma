Hysiac GST — Android update
===========================

Your bills stay on the tablet. Do not uninstall the old app.

1. Copy this Hysiac-GST.apk to the Xiaomi Pad 7.
2. Open it and tap Update / Install. Android keeps the same app and its books.
3. Open Hysiac GST. Invoices, purchases, and stock should still be there.

If Android says the package conflicts, the old app was removed or signed differently.
In that case restore from Settings → Export JSON (do that export BEFORE uninstalling).

Optional safety copy before updating: Settings → Export JSON. That file is your backup.
